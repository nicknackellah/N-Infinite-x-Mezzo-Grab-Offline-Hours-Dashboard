Plan to cut offline hours

Chronic Fix this week

These branches never opened on Grab during their scheduled hours.

  • Call each branch manager: is the store trading, and is the Grab Merchant app or tablet switched on?
  • If the store is closed or not doing delivery, set it to temporarily closed or remove the hours so it stops counting as offline.
  • If it is trading, fix the device: dedicated tablet on charge, auto-accept on, logged in before opening.
  • Re-check next week's report; escalate any branch still dark to the brand owner.

Occasional Find the cause

Full-day or partial outages on a few days.

  • Ask why the store was dark on those dates (staff shortage, mall closure, device issue).
  • Add a daily opening checklist: open the Grab app at shop open and confirm it shows online.
  • Watch next week to confirm the fix holds.

Schedule glitch Clean up hours

Stores scheduled for only 1–5 minutes, which shows as 100% offline.

  • Review opening hours in the Grab Merchant portal for these branches.
  • Remove stray time slots, such as a shift ending one minute after midnight.
  • Small minutes, but they add avoidable offline flags to the store record.

Lost sales are not estimated: this brand has no sales-per-hour baseline in N-Infinite's BigQuery tables. Status rules: Chronic = in the report on 5+ days at 85%+ offline rate; Schedule glitch = every scheduled slot is 5 minutes or less; Occasional = everything else.
